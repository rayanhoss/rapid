**Add 1–2 new posts per month.** More content = more keyword coverage = more traffic.

### Backlinks (Most Powerful Ranking Factor)
Get other websites to link to yours:

- [ ] **Local Philadelphia news sites**: Send press releases about notable projects
- [ ] **Neighborhood Facebook groups**: Engage authentically, share useful tips
- [ ] **Supplier websites**: Ask lumber yards, tile showrooms to list you as a contractor
- [ ] **Local chamber of commerce**: Join Delaware County Chamber of Commerce
- [ ] **Subcontractors**: Ask electricians/plumbers you work with to link to you
- [ ] **Local blog posts**: "Best contractors in Philadelphia" type roundups
