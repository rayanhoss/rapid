## Logo System

### Primary Logo
- Orange square (40×40px minimum) containing white "RR" monogram
- Displayed alongside full name in two lines: "RAPID RENOVATION" (Bebas Neue, large) over "& Demolition LLC" (Barlow Condensed, small, orange)

### Monogram / Icon
- "RR" in white on brand-orange background
- Use for favicon, app icons, social media profile photos, embossed/embroidered marks

### Clear Space
- Minimum clear space around logo = height of the "R" letterform on all sides
- Never place logo on a busy image without a dark overlay or solid background

### Do Not
- Stretch or distort the logo
- Use on backgrounds with insufficient contrast
- Recreate using non-brand fonts
- Use the orange wordmark on orange background

- **Orange on dark backgrounds:** ✅ Always works
- **White on orange:** ✅ Use for button text, inverted sections

- **Dark on dark:** ❌ Never use steel on steel — always ensure contrast ratio ≥ 4.5:1

## Typography

### Display Font — Bebas Neue
- **Use for:** Hero headings, page titles, section headers, stat numbers
- **Style:** All caps, wide tracking (`letter-spacing: 0.05em` or `tracking-wider`)
- **Never use for:** Body copy, small text below 24px

### Condensed Font — Barlow Condensed
- **Weights:** 400, 500, 600, 700
- **Use for:** Navigation, buttons, labels, badges, subheadings, category tags
- **Style:** Uppercase with wide tracking for labels (`tracking-widest`), sentence case for longer subheads
- **Never use for:** Long paragraph text

### Body Font — Barlow
- **Weights:** 300 (light), 400 (regular), 500 (medium), 600 (semibold)
- **Use for:** All paragraph text, descriptions, form labels, footer links
- **Line height:** 1.6–1.8 for body copy
- **Never use:** Below 14px for body copy

### Type Scale

## Imagery

### Photography Style
- **Lighting:** High-contrast, natural light preferred. Avoid flat, overcast shots.
- **Subject matter:** Real job sites, finished work, crew in action, before/after
- **Color treatment:** Desaturate slightly, increase contrast — makes images feel more editorial
- **Avoid:** Stock-looking staged photos, overly polished lifestyle imagery

### Image Overlays
- Always apply a dark overlay (30–60% opacity) over hero background images
- Use gradient overlays (steel to transparent) for section backgrounds
- Ensure text contrast ≥ 4.5:1 over any image

### Before/After Documentation
- Shoot from identical positions (same angle, focal length)
- Consistent lighting conditions
- Clean, uncluttered frame
- Label with location and service type

## Voice & Tone

### Brand Voice Attributes
- **Direct** — Say it plainly. "We demolish walls" not "We facilitate spatial transformation"
- **Confident** — State facts, not qualifiers. "We're insured" not "We might be able to help"
- **Human** — Real language, not corporate-speak
- **Local** — Reference Philadelphia, Upper Darby, Lansdowne naturally

### Headline Formula
Strong verbs + specificity:
- ✅ "Kitchen gutted and rebuilt in 8 days"
- ✅ "Philadelphia's trusted demolition crew"
- ✅ "Build. Break. Transform."
- ❌ "Your premier renovation solution provider"
- ❌ "We take pride in our quality workmanship"

### CTAs
- Primary: "Get a Free Quote" / "Request an Estimate"
- Phone: "Call 347-977-3212"
- Secondary: "View Our Work" / "See the Project"
